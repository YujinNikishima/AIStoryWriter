# Narrative style

Write in the language set in `project.json`. These instructions can remain in English.

- Prioritize engaging prose and coherent scenes over checklists and event logs.
- Keep character voice and behavior true to approved characterization, while allowing earned growth.
- Use natural paragraphs, varied sentence rhythms, motivated dialogue and believable reactions.
- Match loud punctuation with the narrated emotional and physical action.
- Avoid repetitive rhetorical patterns, sterile mechanics recaps, and unnecessary restatements.
- Do not replace story scenes with tables of inventory or progression.
- When an established fact conflicts with a draft, the draft must be corrected or the conflict escalated. Never quietly retcon an approved chapter.
- Do not invent a previously unseen secret, ability or historical event solely to solve a continuity problem.

Any requested per-story tone, point of view, genre or restrictions take precedence when approved.
